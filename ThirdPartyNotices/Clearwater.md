# Clearwater

Source: https://github.com/Aureliengmz/clearwater
Upstream revision read for this work: 4bc826134321043a25df3c2b6fed16fb7b9241e8.

Used as the source/reference for the reduced spectral waves and baked caustics in
`SourceAssets/ClearwaterWater20260926` and `SourceAssets/ClearwaterNative20260926`.
