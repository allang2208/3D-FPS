# Hundred-eyed Slag production inputs

This publication contains project-authored gameplay code, authoring recipes, HLSL and prose. It does not publish the model, texture, animation, reference-image, Unreal package, sampled-pose or Blender payloads listed below.

- The creature was produced from the user's chosen Meshy multi-image workflow. Model and PBR outputs, account/task receipts and credentials remain local; API credentials are read from the environment and are excluded from publication.
- The arm-motion donor is the user's imported Epic Paragon Rampage content. Listing: <https://www.fab.com/listings/0807cf74-08fd-4a33-8c8d-f33c9439fb1f>. Donor meshes, skin weights, animations and sampled transforms remain local. Availability without a purchase price is not treated here as permission to redistribute the original files.
- Eye-charge authoring reuses locally acquired MayuOrbs and ElectricMagic material/texture/emitter inputs. Smoke authoring uses the locally available Niagara Examples emitter and the project's Mantaflow density atlas. None of those original packages or images are included in this source publication.
- Unreal Engine basic-shape and Niagara APIs are referenced by paths/API calls. The engine and its plugins are not bundled.

An authorized local asset installation is required before restoring the effect or recreating animation exports. See `Docs/Monsters/hundred-eyed-slag-publication-20261002.md` and `Docs/AssetSetup.md` for the recovery boundary and retained inputs. No additional license or public redistribution permission for those inputs is asserted by this document.
