# Gunplay FX source

Original procedural flash and smoke HLSL plus runtime animation, authored for this FPSGAME project on 2026-09-09. No third-party art downloads are used.

Geometry uses Unreal Engine BasicShapes Plane/Cylinder, provided with the engine under its applicable license. The project-owned materials can be regenerated with Tools/AssetPipeline/build_gunplay_fx.py.
