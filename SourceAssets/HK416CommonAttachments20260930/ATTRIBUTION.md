# HK416 common attachment adaptation

HK416 factory geometry, texture atlases, original magazine, grip and stock are derived from **HK416 Full ReWorked**, by **MojoLeeDa**, Sketchfab model `669a9ee17dc44580b53425a08c2f83d0`, **CC BY 4.0**. Source URL: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/hk416-full-reworked-669a9ee17dc44580b53425a08c2f83d0. Original archive, license evidence and authoring inventory remain in `../HK416Reworked20260930`.

Changes: detachable factory sections, target-specific interfaces, a patterned curved extended magazine, factory feed tower fitted to the accepted common drum, receiver-matched coating tiles and common attachments fitted to the HK416 reference frame.

Common attachment bodies and their original UV/material bindings are reused from the project's existing production assets; exact per-part asset paths and exported source FBX paths are recorded in `../M16UniversalAttachments20260920/sources.json` and this batch's `models.json`. These retain their respective original asset licenses. Reuse here does not grant redistribution rights for source assets or apply the HK416 license to them.

Animation source: existing accepted M4 grouped hand poses and drum contact actions, adapted to HK416's current V7 bare-arm rig and mechanical timeline. Source details remain in `author_animations.py` and `animations.json`. Infima rig/animation and V7 arm licenses remain separate from the Sketchfab model license.

Framed modification icons: five images produced using built-in imagegen, with this batch's actual part silhouettes and `../FirearmFramedIcons20260930/muzzle_brake_framed_v2.png` as the framing reference. Prompts and original output filenames are recorded in `icon_generation.json`; project copies are in `FramedIcons`. Category icons reuse the corresponding physical factory-part images.

No gameplay tests or acceptance previews were run for this batch.
