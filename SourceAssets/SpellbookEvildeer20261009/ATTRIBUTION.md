- `original-source.zip` is the unchanged download. `Original` contains its extracted FBX and textures; additional `.fbm` files were extracted by Blender from the FBX.
- The actual archive contains FBX and textures; no original `.blend` was supplied. The local `.blend` files are conversions/adaptations made for this project.

Selected model: source `book2`, the blue-purple **Elemental Alchemy** cover with gold ornament. The exterior geometry and its original UV layout and texture maps are retained. Project modifications include normalization to centimetres and a spine pivot, splitting the book at its middle page plane, adding illustrated inner surfaces using textures from the same set, three thin skinned page leaves, and opening/closing/forward/backward page-turn animations.
