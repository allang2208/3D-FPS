# 握把设计参考生成提示词

生成器：本次会话内置图像生成。三款分别生成同一部件的正面、侧面和背面参考，模型由 Blender 按原剑接口重建。

## shock_wrap

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a precise 3D game-asset design reference sheet of ONE detachable sword grip, the shock-absorbing wrapped grip for an icy blue crystal greatsword. Three consistent orthographic views of the SAME part: front, right side, back, evenly spaced on a plain light gray background. Grip only: no blade, no crossguard, no pommel, no hands. Top and bottom each have a short simple antique bronze mounting ferrule with a narrow brushed silver ring; the long middle is dark desaturated blue-gray padded leather with a clean continuous crisscross helical wrap, subtle leather grain and recessed seams. Compact oval cross-section, gently tapered ergonomic waist, no huge protrusions or gems. Total mounting-face length 17.7 cm, approximately 3 cm wide; reproduce the same proportions in all views. The outer wrap should look tactile and soft, modest raised overlapping edges. Grounded realistic fantasy craftsmanship, restrained ornament, readable modeling shapes, soft even studio lighting, complete part visible with generous margins. No labels, no arrows, no watermark. This is a design reference for exact Blender reconstruction, not a whole sword.
