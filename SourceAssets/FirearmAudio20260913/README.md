Source publication: https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library

Recorded by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and Matthew Nanney. The publication explicitly releases this library under CC0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Selected prepared WAV files were downloaded from the same library's mirror at https://github.com/petroulacl/fps-asset-kit/tree/main/sfx/firearm_sfx/Prepared%20SFX%20Library because the original full archive transfer was impractically slow. The interrupted full archive was discarded. `Original/` contains the selected recordings; source hashes and output mappings are in `provenance.json`.

`prepare.py` authors 4 normal and 4 suppressed variations for each rifle as 48 kHz mono PCM16. M4 uses four AR-15 shots with transient/body filtering and shortened outdoor decay. QBZ191 blends AR-15 attack with a restrained AK-47 mid/low body. Suppressed versions are filtered, quieter designs from those sources. Neither QBZ191 nor suppressed variants are claimed as recordings of those actual configurations. No external commercial audio or game extracts are used.

Import with `Tools/AssetPipeline/import_firearm_audio_20260913.py` into `/Game/Weapons/FreeFirearmAudio20260913`. Character initialization selects the M4 or QBZ191 bank; firing selects a non-repeating variant and permits natural overlapping short tails, with a six-voice owner limit and 20 ms voice-steal fade. Existing gameplay shot timing and ammunition remain authoritative. M1911 and AKM playback are outside this revision.

The user should restart the editor after the native build, then compare M4 and QBZ191 single shots, short bursts, sustained fire and suppressors. Audio was authored and imported; no listening test or game acceptance was performed by the agent.
