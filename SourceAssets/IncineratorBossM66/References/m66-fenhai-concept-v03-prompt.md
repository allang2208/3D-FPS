## 完整提示词

Use case: stylized-concept / creature redesign with supplied series reference images.
Create one exceptional, frightening, production-readable AAA survival horror CREATURE DESIGN SHEET, landscape 3:2, high resolution. Redesign M-66 焚骸, a BOSS specimen in an abandoned underground biological research facility's incineration district.
Image 1 is the previous M66 design to REPLACE structurally and materially. Images 2-5 are the project's real M-series design language references: Hundred Eyed Slag (charred bone crust, unsettling real eyes), M07 (pale vascular skin, anatomical membranes, restraint tag), M14 (twisted living tissue, biological apertures), M27 (anatomical structure and facility restraints). The new creature belongs convincingly to the SAME roster, but has its OWN instantly readable silhouette. DO NOT copy a reference creature or make a collage of their body parts.

Core new design: a massive, HEADLESS, TWO-LEGGED upright living ash furnace. A tall, wide, extremely asymmetrical pear-shaped thoracic kiln sac dominates the silhouette, bulging up behind the shoulders like an overinflated distorted lung. Height about 3.8 m. Clearly visible TWO powerful distorted humanoid legs underneath, knees separated and bent under the weight, splayed thick fleshy toes. Only these two feet bear weight. TWO arms hang at different lengths; the heavier arm has four long blunt, tong-like fleshy fingers curving inward, the smaller arm is contracted across the lower belly. Hands never serve as walking legs. Very deliberate negative spaces between arms, body and legs. The silhouette should feel heavy, unstable and disturbingly alive, NOT heroic, athletic, demonic or a tree-man.

Iconic anatomy: no ordinary human face, no skull mask, no conventional neck. On the front upper torso is a disproportionately deep vertically elongated soot-black INTAKE opening, enclosed by three interlocking pairs of thick fleshy valve-lips that close like the valves of a diseased organ, with short irregular inward pointing calcified teeth. The opening extends from upper chest to above the pelvis. This is a natural anatomical orifice, not a torn wound, and not a metal machine door. Deep inside it: layers of wrinkled gray filtering membranes and a tiny dim orange-red ember deep at the bottom; dark cavities, readable subtle heat. Around ONLY the upper left side of the opening is a compact crooked cluster of 7-9 cloudy, wet, differently sized eyes that seem to stare in inconsistent directions. No centered giant eyeball, no even all-over eye polka dots. The upper right shoulder swells into a closed gray blistery lung sac. On the hunched upper back three UNEVEN folded fleshy exhalation chimneys point obliquely upward, their lips blackened and crusted. They are living gill-like vents, not metal pipes. A small ash stream rises from only one vent.

Materials unify with M series: dominant pale gray-pink translucent skin, thick ivory sinews, blue-purple branching subcutaneous veins, polished damp areas at fold junctions, rough gray ash sediment at lower edges; dry dark brown-black calcified soot crust is localized around intake lips, back vents and forearm extremities, about 20 percent surface coverage. Small rusted steel restraint cuffs and an old rectangular off-white identification plate clearly marked M-66 embedded in a short strap on the upper torso. Metal is minor, not armor. Biological skin is continuous and believable. Terrifying body proportions and displaced sensory organs, oppressive specimen presence. No gratuitous blood, no gore, no exposed human victims. No robes, no ragged cloth cloak, no shroud, no elegant wraith face, no fantasy crown, no lava golem, no huge flames, no symmetric angel wings, no quadruped or spider silhouette, no six-legged stance.

A small unobtrusive 1.75 m human SILHOUETTE for scale beside a 3.8 m creature silhouette in the lower margin; this is a diagram, not a victim.
