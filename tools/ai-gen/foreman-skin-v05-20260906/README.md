# Foreman V05 skin correction

Input: `../foreman-motion-v04-20260906/foreman-motion-v04.blend`.
Rebuild with Blender 5.1: `blender -b --python build_skin.py`.

Original Attack and Howl contact sheets were inspected before editing. Attack
retains right overhead windup, left balancing arm, forward left support step,
low whip contact and recovery. Its 39 source frames span 1.5 s; zero-based
frame 21 begins at 0.59625 s. Howl remains 24 frames / 3 s. Existing body
animation channels, bone rests, UVs and source geometry are preserved.

Shoulder and elbow weights diffuse only along mesh edges inside a bounded
region. No nearest-spatial-neighbour transfer between disconnected surfaces.
Weights are pruned to four and normalized. Under the same isolated 90-degree
right shoulder test, selected edge stretch p99 falls from 4.559 to 1.982;
edges stretched over 3x fall from 248 to 31. These metrics demonstrate an
improvement, not perfect deformation at every extreme. Front torso core
remains unaffected by the isolated arm poses.

Two new fingers_cup controls provide a modest collective four-finger curl:
right hand holds a tighter pose, left hand opens during attack, and both
relax during death. This is not a complete per-phalange or thumb rig.

The previous 33 independent unit-scale whip section bones are preserved.
Export skin validation samples 1349 times across all five clips, retaining
the 2.883 m maximum whip distance and all original durations. The candidate
passes the existing eight engine motion checks (including loop continuity,
joint steps and grounded displacement). Full runtime evidence and previews
are generated by render_motion.gd using project-default rendering.

Files: editable foreman-skin-v05.blend, self-contained GLB, repeatable builder,
controlled-pose audit, skin metrics and runtime previews. Audit render colors
show weight regions; these colors do not replace the original game materials.

Integration: scenes/enemies/foreman_zombie.tscn now loads
assets/models/foreman_zombie/foreman_v05.glb. Formal model: 24 behavior checks
and 8 motion checks passed; main 180-frame headless smoke passed. Generic
combat/reload assertions pass but their existing warehouse fixture still
reports a missing WarehousePanel. Behavior teardown reports existing resource
leak warnings; these runs are not claimed to have entirely clean logs.
Default-renderer previews completed for Idle, Walk, Attack and Death, plus
game-speed Walk. Attack remains 1500 ms; Death preview includes a 1000 ms hold.
